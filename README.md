## Project Overview & Achievement
Byte Solutions was awarded 2nd Place out of 12 student consulting teams in a competitive, semester-long Request for Proposal (RFP) simulation for Maddon & Associates (M&A). M&A sought a $10,000,000 technology investment to modernize the education-to-career pipeline, support multi-stakeholder collaboration, and deliver measurable ROI within 24 to 36 months.

Our team designed Longevity, an AI-powered, cloud-hosted SaaS/PaaS platform that guides individuals through their entire professional journey—from high school selection through university, skill development, internship matching, and long-term career growth.

## Proposed System Architecture & Core Features

* **Trait Tracker & Employer Matching:** AI-driven engine aligning student personality traits and skills against high-performing employee profiles.
* **Eisenhower Priority Matrix:** Dynamic task management tool prioritizing academic responsibilities, urgent milestones, and long-term career goals.
* **Knowledge Management & Community Builder:** Integrated alumni network supporting mentorship, active recruiting, and continuous learning.
* **Cloud Architecture:** Hybrid SaaS/PaaS/IaaS infrastructure engineered for enterprise scalability, security, and data governance.
